# Assumptions for the business sizing

Generated by `src/sizing.py`. Do not edit by hand. Money is in Brazilian reais (R$) of order value
(item price plus freight) paid by customers.

## Formula

Repeat value lost per quarter = late first orders in the quarter
x drop in the 180-day repeat probability caused by being late
x order value a repeat customer brings back within 180 days.

## Assumptions

1. **Base quarter is 2018 Q2.** It is the last complete quarter. It had
   19,643 delivered orders, a late rate of 4.2% and
   799 late first orders. Quarters with a higher late rate would give a larger number.
2. **The effect is the RD estimate for repeat purchase within 180 days** (first orders, h = 4):
   +0.1 pp, 95% CI -2.0 pp to
   +2.2 pp. Low, base and high use the favourable CI bound, the point
   estimate and the harmful CI bound.
3. **Scope "local" applies the effect only to first orders 1 to 4 days late** (313
   in the quarter). This is where the RD identifies it.
4. **Scope "all late" applies the same effect to every late first order.** This is an extrapolation. Orders
   that are many days late probably lose more, which this cannot measure.
5. **Value per repeat customer is R$ 162:** the average order value of all
   purchases made within 180 days by the 1,109 first-order customers who came back.
6. **Only the first-order effect is counted.** Repeat customers who get a late order are not included,
   because the RD sample is first orders only.
7. **The effect of a bad review on future sales is not counted.** The data has no link between a review
   and later demand for that seller or product, so it cannot be computed.
8. **Order value is not Olist revenue.** Olist earns a commission that is not in the data. Multiply by
   the commission rate to get Olist's share.

## Results per quarter

| Scenario | Effect | Local scope | All late scope |
|:--|--:|--:|--:|
| Low | +2.2 pp | R$ -1,115 | R$ -2,846 |
| Base | +0.1 pp | R$ -57 | R$ -146 |
| High | -2.0 pp | R$ 1,000 | R$ 2,554 |
| Naive gap (not causal, reference) | -0.4 pp | | R$ 532 |

A negative number means the scenario implies late orders gain repeat value. The low and base rows
are negative or close to zero because the estimate cannot be told apart from zero.

## Reading

Even the high scenario, applied to every late first order, is R$ 2,554 per quarter.
That is 0.08% of the quarter's order value (R$ 3,273,102).
Repeat purchase is rare on Olist (2.0% within 180 days), so even a large relative
effect on it is a small amount of money. The bigger cost of late delivery is in reviews, which this data
cannot price.

## Exposure figures used in the recommendations (2018 Q2)

- Order value of all late orders: R$ 149,463 (820 orders).
- Order value of orders 3 or more days late: R$ 118,724 (644 orders).
- Orders whose review survey was sent before delivery: 576
  (order value R$ 106,092).
- 5 states have a late rate at least double the national 6.8%
  (AL, MA, SE, PI, CE). Together they have a late rate of 15.7%, and hold
  7.7% of late orders but only 3.3% of all orders.
