# Data profile

Generated by `src/profile_data.py` from the raw Kaggle files. Do not edit by hand.

## Limitations that affect the analysis

1. **The data ends on 2018-08-29.** Only 20 orders were placed after August 2018 and none of them were delivered. Any metric that needs follow-up time (repeat purchase, retention, seller activity) is right censored near the end. Every such metric in this project only uses customers or sellers with the full window observed.
2. **2016 is almost empty.** 329 orders were placed in 2016, with a gap in November and December. Cohort tables start in January 2017.
3. **customer_id is not a customer.** There are 99,441 customer_id values but only 96,096 customer_unique_id values. customer_id is created per order, so using it would make every customer look like a one-time buyer.
4. **Reviews for late orders are mostly sent before the parcel arrives.** For late orders, 74.5% of reviews were created before the delivery date, against 0.4% for on-time orders. Olist appears to send the survey around the estimated date. A late order's review often rates a parcel that has not arrived yet.
5. **Status and timestamps disagree for a few orders.** 8 orders are 'delivered' with no delivery date, and 6 orders that are not 'delivered' do have one. The analysis grain requires both status and date.
6. **Some orders have more than one review** (547 orders), and 789 review_id values are attached to more than one order. The model keeps one review per order (the latest answered).
7. **775 orders have no items.** They are mostly unavailable or canceled orders and cannot be given a category.
8. **610 products have no category** and 2 Portuguese categories have no English translation. Both are labelled explicitly rather than dropped.
9. **The seller funnel is small.** 8,000 leads, 842 closed deals, and only 380 closed deals ever made a sale in the orders data. Leads were first contacted between 2017-06-14 and 2018-05-31, so the latest deals have little time to sell before the data ends.
10. **There is no cost, margin or commission data.** Money is measured as order value (item price plus freight) paid by customers. Olist's own revenue share cannot be computed from this data.
11. **There is no data on what customers were shown or told** (delivery messages, notifications, marketing). Any intervention effect has to be tested, not estimated, from this data.
12. **Geolocation is not used.** It has 1,000,163 rows for 19,015 zip prefixes, with many coordinates per prefix. State is used instead.
